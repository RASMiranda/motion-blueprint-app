Training Guide

Rate of Perceived Exertion
7
RPE
Drag the dial. 6–8 is the target zone for most working sets — hard, but with reps left in reserve.
Supersets
Two exercises back-to-back, little to no rest between. Saves time and keeps intensity high. Feeling too gassed to hold form? Straight sets (finish all sets of one move, then the next) work just as well — pick what fits your energy.
Progressive Overload
Each week, try to add a little more weight, 1 more rep, or 1 more set than last time. You don't need to progress everything at once — small, steady gains compound over months.
RPE Reference
RPE 1–4 — Very easy to light. Warm-ups, technique work.
RPE 5–6 — Moderate. 4–5 reps left in reserve.
RPE 7–8 — Hard. 2–3 reps left in reserve. (Target zone)
RPE 9–10 — Max effort. 0–1 reps left in reserve.
Workout Flow
Every session follows: Warm-Up → Power → Strength Supersets → Finisher. The first 4 warm-up steps never change; Prehab & Core rotate by day.
